Our story

Roasted with patience, served with love

Roast & Co. started as a tiny roaster in a garage. Today we are a neighbourhood coffee bar, but the idea is still the same: great beans, honest prices and time for a chat.

  • Direct trade

    We buy straight from farmers and pay above fair-trade prices.

  • Roasted weekly

    Small batches, so your coffee is always fresh.

  • Zero-waste bar

    Bring your own cup and get €0,30 off.
